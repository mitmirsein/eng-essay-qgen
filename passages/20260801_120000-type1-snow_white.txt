There was a queen who wanted a beautiful child. She always prayed that she had a daughter that had skin as white as snow. Some time later, the queen dies when she gave birth to a baby daughter, and the daughter is Snow White. A year later, Snow White's father, the king, marries again. His new wife is very beautiful. The new queen has a magic mirror, and she asks every morning, "Mirror mirror on the wall, who is the most beautiful one of all?" The mirror always tells the queen that she is the most beautiful. The queen is always pleased with that response. But when Snow White is seven years old, she becomes more beautiful than the queen. So when the queen again asks her mirror the same question, it tells her that Snow White is the most beautiful.
